# Summary Agent

### Intelligent Underwriting Report Generation & Decision Support

**Part of the InsureIQ Multi-Agent Underwriting Platform**

---

# Overview

The Summary Agent is the final AI agent in the InsureIQ underwriting workflow. Built using **UiPath Agent Builder**, it consolidates the outputs generated by the Claim History Agent, Financial Risk Agent, and Operational Risk Agent into a single underwriting assessment.

Rather than performing new risk calculations, the Summary Agent focuses on interpreting the findings produced by the research agents and presenting them in a clear, structured, and explainable format. It transforms multiple independent assessments into a comprehensive underwriting report that enables underwriters to quickly understand the applicant's overall risk profile.

By eliminating the need to manually review multiple reports, the Summary Agent reduces decision-making time while ensuring that every recommendation is supported by evidence collected throughout the underwriting process.

---

# Purpose

The primary objective of the Summary Agent is to answer the final underwriting question:

> **Based on all available information, what is the overall risk profile of this submission, and what recommendation should be presented to the underwriter?**

The Summary Agent combines the outputs of all specialised research agents and generates a single underwriting report that supports informed business decisions.

---

# Where the Agent Fits

The Summary Agent is executed after all three research agents have completed their assessments.

Once the Claim History Agent, Financial Risk Agent, and Operational Risk Agent finish their analysis, their outputs are collected from the UiPath Storage Bucket and passed to the Summary Agent.

The agent consolidates these findings into a unified report, which is then presented to the underwriter during the Review Stage before the case is closed.

# Analysis Performed

The Summary Agent does not generate new risk assessments. Instead, it consolidates and interprets the outputs generated by the specialised research agents.

---

## Consolidating Risk Assessments

The agent reviews the findings produced by:

- Claim History Agent
- Financial Risk Agent
- Operational Risk Agent

Each assessment contributes to the applicant's overall underwriting profile.

---

## Executive Summary Generation

Using the information received from all research agents, the Summary Agent prepares a concise executive summary highlighting the most important underwriting observations.

The summary enables underwriters to quickly understand the applicant's overall risk without reviewing multiple individual reports.

---

## Overall Risk Evaluation

The agent combines individual risk assessments to determine an overall underwriting risk profile.

Example:

| Assessment | Risk Level |
|------------|------------|
| Claim History | Medium |
| Financial | Low |
| Operational | Medium |

Overall Assessment

**Medium Risk**

---

## Recommendation Generation

Based on the combined findings, the Summary Agent prepares underwriting recommendations that assist the underwriter during policy evaluation.

Recommendations are intended as decision support rather than automated policy decisions.

# Business Value

The Summary Agent serves as the final intelligence layer within the InsureIQ platform by transforming multiple independent risk assessments into a single, structured underwriting report.

Instead of requiring underwriters to review separate outputs from multiple agents, it presents a unified view of the applicant's historical claims, financial stability, operational exposure, and overall underwriting risk.

This consolidated approach improves decision consistency, reduces manual effort, shortens underwriting turnaround time, and enables underwriters to focus on making informed business decisions supported by transparent AI-generated insights.
